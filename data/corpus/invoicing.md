# Invoicing

Acme Invoicing lets you send invoices that customers can pay by card or bank transfer. Acme charges 0.5% for each paid invoice, in addition to the normal payment processing fee. Unpaid invoices are free.

You can set a due date of up to 90 days after the invoice date. Acme sends automatic reminder emails 3 days before the due date and on the due date.

Customers pay through a hosted invoice page, so you do not need to build a payment form.
