# Plans

Acme Payments offers three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise.

- The Starter plan has no monthly fee and includes 1 team seat.
- The Growth plan costs $49 per month and includes 10 team seats, custom checkout branding, and priority email support.
- The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, unlimited team seats, a dedicated account manager, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.

You can upgrade or downgrade a plan at any time, and changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
