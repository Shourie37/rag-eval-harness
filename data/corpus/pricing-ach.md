# ACH bank debit pricing

ACH Direct Debit lets customers pay directly from a US bank account. Acme charges 0.8% per ACH debit, and the fee is capped at $5 per transaction.

ACH credit transfers that customers push to your account cost a flat $1 each. A failed ACH debit costs $4 because the bank returns the payment.

ACH payments usually settle in 4 business days, which is slower than card payments.
