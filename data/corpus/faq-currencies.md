# Currencies FAQ

Acme can charge customers in 42 currencies. Merchants can receive payouts in 12 settlement currencies, including USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, and AUD.

If you charge in a currency that is not one of your settlement currencies, Acme converts the funds and applies the 1% conversion fee. Exchange rates are locked at the moment of the charge.

Amounts in the API are always given in the smallest currency unit, so 10 US dollars is sent as 1000.
