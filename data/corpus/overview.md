# Acme Payments overview

Acme Payments is a fictional online payments platform for small and mid-sized businesses. It lets businesses accept card payments, bank debits, invoices, and subscriptions through one API and one dashboard.

Acme Payments was founded in 2019 and processes payments for more than 40,000 merchants. The platform is available to businesses in 18 countries and can charge customers in 42 currencies.

Every account includes a test mode that mirrors live mode without moving real money. Merchants manage their account, view balances, and download reports from the Acme Dashboard.
