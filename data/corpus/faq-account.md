# Account setup FAQ

Anyone with a registered business or sole proprietorship can create an Acme account from the sign-up page. To activate live payments, you must verify your identity and add a bank account for payouts.

Acme asks for a government-issued ID, your business tax number, and your bank details. Most account verifications finish within 2 business days. You can use test mode right away, before verification is complete.

Each account can have one owner, and the owner can invite team members based on the plan.
