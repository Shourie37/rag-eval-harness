# Closing an account

You can close an Acme account from the Account settings page in the dashboard. Before you close the account, Acme pays out the remaining balance to your bank account.

Acme keeps a reserve for 90 days after closure to cover late refunds and disputes. Closed accounts cannot be reopened, so you must create a new account to process payments again.

Acme keeps transaction records for 7 years to meet legal requirements.
