# Refund policy

You can refund a charge up to 180 days after the original payment. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Acme does not return the original processing fee when you issue a refund. Card refunds usually take 5 to 10 business days to appear on the customer statement.

If your Acme balance is too low to cover a refund, Acme debits the bank account linked to your account. A refund cannot be cancelled after its status becomes succeeded.
