# Supported countries FAQ

Acme Payments is available to businesses in 18 countries. These countries include the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Australia, New Zealand, and Japan.

Your business must have a legal entity and a bank account in a supported country. Customers can pay you from almost any country, even if the business cannot open an account there.

Acme plans to launch in new countries based on merchant demand.
