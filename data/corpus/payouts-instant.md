# Instant payouts

Instant payouts send money to an eligible debit card in about 30 minutes, including weekends and holidays.

Instant payouts cost 1% of the payout amount, with a minimum fee of $0.50. Each instant payout can be at most $10,000.

Instant payouts are available only after an account has processed payments for 30 days. Not every bank supports instant payouts, so the dashboard shows which debit cards are eligible.
