# Subscriptions

Acme Billing lets you charge customers on a recurring schedule, such as monthly or yearly. Acme Billing costs 0.7% of recurring billing volume, in addition to the payment processing fee.

You can offer a free trial of up to 60 days before the first charge. When a recurring payment fails, Smart Retries tries the card again up to 4 times over 2 weeks.

You can let customers upgrade, downgrade, or cancel their subscription through the hosted customer portal. Proration is applied automatically when a customer changes plans in the middle of a billing period.
