# Fraud protection

Every Acme account includes Acme Shield, a machine learning system that scores each payment for fraud risk. Acme Shield is included at no extra cost on all plans.

Shield Advanced adds custom fraud rules and manual review queues for $0.05 per screened transaction. Acme blocks payments with a risk score above 85 by default, and you can change this threshold in Shield Advanced.

Acme also supports 3D Secure authentication, which can shift fraud liability to the card issuer.
