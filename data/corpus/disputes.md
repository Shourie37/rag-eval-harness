# Disputes and chargebacks

A dispute happens when a cardholder asks their bank to reverse a charge. Acme charges a $15 dispute fee for each dispute. The dispute fee is returned to you if you win the dispute.

You have 9 days to submit evidence after Acme notifies you of a new dispute. Useful evidence includes delivery tracking, customer emails, and signed receipts.

The card network makes the final decision, which can take up to 75 days.
