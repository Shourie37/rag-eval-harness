# Standard card pricing

Acme Payments charges 2.9% + 30 cents for each successful domestic card charge. There are no setup fees, monthly fees, or hidden fees on the standard plan.

International cards cost an additional 1.5% on top of the domestic rate. When a charge requires currency conversion, Acme adds a 1% conversion fee.

Failed charges are free and are not billed. The standard rate applies to Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover cards.
